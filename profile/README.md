# Bharat AI Mission

### Democratizing AI for a Billion Dreams

Bharat AI Mission is an open-source initiative working to make artificial intelligence more accessible through collaboration, education, research, and reusable AI resources.

We bring together developers, students, researchers, educators, startups, institutions, and industry professionals to build AI solutions that benefit India and the wider world.

## What We're Building

- **Open AI Tools:** Reusable tools, frameworks, and reference implementations.
- **Bharat AI Commons:** Indian-language resources, datasets, and AI evaluation benchmarks.
- **AI Builders Lab:** Hands-on learning projects and opportunities for first-time contributors.
- **Enterprise AI:** Open architecture patterns, agentic AI examples, and responsible AI practices.

## Who Can Contribute?

Everyone has a role to play. You can contribute code, research, documentation, testing, translations, design, ideas, and domain expertise.

## How to Get Involved

1. Explore our repositories and identify a project that interests you.
2. Review its README and contribution guidelines.
3. Look for issues labelled `good first issue` or `help wanted`.
4. Ask questions, contribute, and collaborate with the community.
